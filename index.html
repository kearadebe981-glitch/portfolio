About me

I'm a third-year Software Development student who enjoys the technical side of building software, but I'm drawn most to where technology meets the business problem it's solving. I'd rather sit with a stakeholder and work out what a system actually needs to do than just write code in isolation, which is part of why I'm steering my career toward business analysis and project management.

Outside the classroom I'm the SRC Head of Academics for ICT at IIE Rosebank College Braamfontein, a role that has sharpened the same skills I bring to project work: coordinating people, tracking progress, and making sure the right things get done on time. I've also served as Class Representative for two years running.

I'm currently looking for an entry-level opportunity, ideally somewhere I can apply technical fundamentals while growing into a more business-facing role.
